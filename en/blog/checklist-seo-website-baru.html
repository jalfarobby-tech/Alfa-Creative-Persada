SEO (Search Engine Optimization) Checklist for a New Website Before Launch

October 28, 20264 min read

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) often sounds complicated, yet most of the basics can be done before a website launches. Here is the checklist we use on every project.

Before going live

  • A unique, clear title for every page, with the main topic and brand name
  • A short description per page that explains its content in one or two sentences
  • One main heading (H1) per page, with subheadings in order below it
  • Original, useful text, not copied from other websites
  • Compressed images with alternative text
  • A comfortable mobile view, since most visitors come from phones
  • Good load speed, especially on mobile networks
  • Clean page addresses, with one main (canonical) version

At and after launch

  • Enable HTTPS
  • Create and submit sitemap.xml
  • Register the site in Google Search Console and request indexing of the home page
  • Create a Google Business Profile so the business shows up in local search and maps
  • Test structured data with Google's Rich Results Test
  • Make sure link previews look good when shared on WhatsApp and social media

What often gets missed

Stuffing in keywords backfires. Write for people first: answer what potential customers actually ask. A frequently asked questions (FAQ) section and short articles are a good way to do that.

Be patient with results

SEO is not an instant button. It usually takes a few weeks to a few months before a new website starts to appear steadily. What you can do is get the foundation right from day one, then add content consistently. The websites we build already cover the technical points above, so you can focus on the content.
