How Much Does a Company Website Cost? The Factors That Decide the Price

October 30, 20264 min read

The question we get most often is about cost. The honest answer: there is no single number that fits everyone. Two websites that are both called a company profile can differ a lot in the work behind them. Here are the factors that matter most, so you can compare quotes fairly.

1. Number and type of pages

A five-page website (home, about, services, portfolio, contact) is clearly different from a twenty-page website with a catalog or articles. The more unique pages, the more design and content needs to be prepared.

2. Design: template or custom

A template saves time and money, but it may look like many other websites. A custom design takes longer because it is tailored to your brand identity. Both are valid, depending on your goal and budget.

3. Features beyond regular pages

  • A contact form connected to email or WhatsApp
  • A blog or product catalog
  • Two languages
  • Integration with Google Maps, Google Sheets, or other services
  • A login system or member area

Every extra feature adds build and testing time.

4. Content: who prepares it

Ready-made text, photos, and logos speed things up. If content has to be written or photographed from scratch, that is separate work and should be priced separately.

5. Domain, hosting, and recurring costs

Domain and hosting are paid yearly and are usually separate from the build cost. Ask early whose name they are registered under. Ideally, yours.

6. SEO and speed

Page structure, titles, descriptions, image size, and load speed affect how easily a website is found on Google. Make sure this is part of the work, not an add-on discussed only at the end.

What to prepare before asking for a quote

  • The goal of the website: look professional, get leads, or sell
  • An estimate of the pages you need
  • Example websites you like
  • Existing logo, photos, and text
  • Your target completion date

With that material, the quotes you receive will be more accurate and easier to compare. At Alfa Creative Persada, the first consultation is free and we send a written estimate of cost and time before work starts.
